FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

HOW CAN WE HELP YOU?

Is StayMatch a property listing website?
No, StayMatch is an inquiry-based platform. Guests send their travel details, and our team finds the best BnB options for them — we don’t list properties publicly.
How do I make an inquiry?
Simply fill out the inquiry form on the homepage with your destination, dates, budget, and preferences. Our team will contact you with suitable options.
Can I pay directly through the website?
Yes, payments can be made via M-Pesa to number 0790535068. Always include your transaction code for verification.
How do I know my booking is confirmed?
Once your payment is verified, you’ll receive a confirmation message and booking details via WhatsApp or email.
Can I become a host or agent?
Yes! During sign-up, select “Host” or “Agent” and provide your ID number. Our admin team will review and approve your account.
Where are your BnBs located?
We operate in Changamwe, Mtwapa, Nyali, and other prime areas across Mombasa and Kenya. Each inquiry is matched to the best available stay.
How can I contact StayMatch support?
You can reach us via WhatsApp (0790535068), email (duncanoteri@gmail.com), or through the Contact page on the website.
