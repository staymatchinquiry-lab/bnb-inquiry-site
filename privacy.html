PRIVACY & POLICY

DATA COLLECTION

StayMatch only collects inquiry details such as your destination, dates, budget, and preferences. We do not publicly list properties or share your personal data with third parties.

PAYMENT SECURITY

All payments are processed securely via M-Pesa (0790535068). Guests must provide a transaction code for verification. No card or bank details are stored on our website.

COMMUNICATION PRIVACY

We respect your privacy. All communication through WhatsApp, email, or phone is confidential and used only to process your inquiry and booking.

DATA PROTECTION

Your information is stored securely and used exclusively for matching you with the best BnB options. We never resell or misuse your data.

INQUIRY-BASED PLATFORM

StayMatch is not a listing website. We operate as an inquiry platform, ensuring personalized service and privacy for both guests and hosts.
