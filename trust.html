TRUST & SAFETY

VERIFIED USERS

All guests, hosts, and agents must provide ID numbers and verified contact details. This ensures accountability and transparency across the StayMatch community.

SECURE INQUIRIES

StayMatch operates as an inquiry platform. Your travel details are handled securely and matched only with verified hosts and agents — never shared publicly.

PAYMENT VERIFICATION

Payments are processed exclusively via M-Pesa with transaction code verification. This prevents fraud and ensures bookings are legitimate.

HOST & AGENT BADGES

Approved hosts and agents receive a verification badge. This badge assures guests that they are dealing with trusted partners.

TRANSPARENCY & REVIEWS

Guests can leave testimonials and ratings after their stay. This feedback system builds trust and helps future guests make informed inquiries.
